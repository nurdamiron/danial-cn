## Processing

Every product photo is normalised to 1200 by 1600. Studio packshots are trimmed
to the subject and recentred on a canvas filled with the sweep's own colour;
environmental shots are smart cropped. Editorial banners are 2400 by 1350.

## Logos

`public/brand/*.svg` are original vector marks drawn by
`scripts/build-logos.mjs`. `pay-kaspi.svg` sets the payment provider's name in
the house alphabet and is not a reproduction of their own logo artwork.
